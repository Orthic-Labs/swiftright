# SR0 bounded source mirror r10

`pack-source-mirror-r10.py` reads only `source-mirror-manifest-r10.json` entries. It rejects reparse inputs, paths outside repo, files over 8 MiB, more than 256 ZIP entries including two manifest records & two admission-source records, or source total over 32 MiB. ZIP carries measured bytes & SHA-256 for every explicit source path, exact source manifest bytes, packer bytes, & admission bytes; pinned production drain helper remains among explicit entries. WorkloadWin, renderer DLL, & PresentMon stay outside ZIP because renderer exceeds per-file archive cap.

Root runs packer with fresh output ZIP, then runs `admit-source-mirror-r10.ps1` with fresh ignored `InstallRoot`, stage, factory, & control roots. Admission verifies every ZIP entry against embedded manifest, installs only under `.factory-work/root-tools`, verifies canonical executable paths & hashes (`7e749248...d930c`, `7578cd5...aeac2`, `b2a706b...f1af`), invokes mirrored stager with `-RepositoryRoot` mirror, invokes mirrored preflight, & writes `source-mirror-admission-r10.json` with `nativeActions=0`.
