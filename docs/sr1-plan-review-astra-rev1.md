Shared font files do not guarantee identical shaping.

8. **MAJOR — Geometry hooks cannot capture “every view.”** [SR1:132](/Volumes/D/claude/swiftright/docs/sr1-plan.md:132). 11. **MAJOR — Animation sampling proves interpolation, not presentation.** [SR1:134](/Volumes/D/claude/swiftright/docs/sr1-plan.md:134). [Apple supports per-frame interpolated writes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/animatable/animatabledata-6nydg), so sampler concept is valid. However, its setter timestamp does not establish displayed-frame time or another view’s effective transaction. Resampling can conceal stalls & mishandle interruption boundaries. **Change:** anchor stimulus/transaction times, preserve missing-frame evidence, compare interruption velocity/completion semantics, bounded interpolation error & actual presentation separately.
